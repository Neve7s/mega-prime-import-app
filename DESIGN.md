## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the authority, technical rigor, and materiality required by an elite importer of architectural finishes and heavy-duty structural interior elements. The design dialect merges **Industrial Modernism** with **Architectural Restraint**: high-density typographic hierarchy, clean geometric discipline, and deliberate tonal precision that mirrors raw steel, polished granite, sintered stone, and refined brass.

### Target Audience & Psychological Response
- **Audience:** Architects, commercial general contractors, high-end interior designers, project specifiers, and procurement managers.
- **Perception:** Extreme structural reliability, logistical competence, uncompromising premium quality, and material authenticity.
- **Experience:** The interface feels engineered rather than merely decorated. It provides the tactile confidence of handling a physical architectural swatch deck combined with the speed and clarity of professional specification software.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural dialogue between raw structural materials and luminous detailing:

- **Primary (`#1E232A` - Anthracite Graphite):** Represents solid structural steel, basalt, and architectural framing. Used for primary typography, structural navigation anchors, high-priority interactive states, and definitive borders.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Industrial Brass/Amber):** Evokes raw copper, architectural brass fittings, and precision construction machinery. Employed for strategic focal points, active state highlights, primary conversion elements, and technical badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Architectural Sunburst):** Reserved for contextual warnings, highlight accents, hover states on secondary assets, and micro-metric emphasis.
- **Neutral Stone (`#6B7280` through `#F3F4F6`):**
  - Polished Stone Canvas (`#F8F9FA` to `#F3F4F6`): Base canvas providing clean, low-fatigue contrast for high-resolution material previews.
  - Sintered Surface White (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated card face, form container, and data-panel background.
  - Mortar Gray (`#E5E7EB` / `#D1D5DB`): Hairline structural dividers and modular grid guides.

### Application Rules
1. Maintain an 80/15/5 distribution rule: 80% polished stone/white and graphite baseline; 15% anthracite structural blocks; 5% concentrated industrial amber accents.
2. Amber elements must never compete across adjacent fields; use it to signify operational completion, technical validation, and key commercial actions.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts structural geometry with high-efficiency text engines:

- **Headlines & Spec Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers an architectural, engineered aesthetic with distinct glyph construction. Its structural geometry mirrors blueprint callouts, technical data plates, and project signage.
- **Body & Data Sheets (`Inter`):** Selected for numerical legibility, dense dimensional arrays, specification tables, and extended corporate narrative. Neutral, robust, and free of optical distortions at small sizes.

### Usage Standards
- Labels and dimensional metadata (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must always render in uppercase with tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.06em` or higher) to establish an architectural blueprint cadence.
- Tabular figures must be enabled via `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` across all inventory, technical specifications, and dimensional displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a modular 8-point structural system, echoing architectural grid drawings and structural engineering templates:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid grid locked within a maximum container width of `1440px`. Gutters set to `2rem` (`gutter-lg`), external margin set to `5rem` (`margin-lg`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid. Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`gutter`), outer padding set to `3rem` (`margin-md`).
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column responsive grid. Gutters compressed to `1rem` (`gutter-sm`), canvas margins fixed to `1.5rem` (`margin`).

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections rely on architectural rhythmic breaks: `2.5rem` (`space-xl`) between related modules, expanding to double-scale increments for macro transitions between finish catalogs and operational parameters.
- Data tables, spec sheets, and catalog matrices use condensed internal padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure critical dimensions, finishes, and SKUs stay within immediate visual scan boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated physically: rather than relying on diffuse or atmospheric software shadows, this design system mimics physical materiality, precision millwork, and stone surface layering.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Foundational Slab):** Raw polished concrete background `#F3F4F6`. Zero shadow, planar reference.
- **Surface Level 1 (Material Modules & Spec Cards):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces defined by a 1px solid perimeter boundary in `#E5E7EB`. Micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Sample & Interactive Hover):** Used when architectural finishes are inspected or cards are focused. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.04)`, with a 1px border shift to `#D1D5DB`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers, Heavy Modals, Blueprint Overlays):** Architectural overlays floating above the structural grid. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.06)`, bounded by a dark structural hairline `#1E232A`.

### Structural Outlines
Hairline boundaries (`1px`) are mandatory across all structural cards, tables, and buttons to reinforce the feeling of precision engineering and laser-cut finishing.

## Shapes

The system implements `Soft` (`roundedness: 1`) geometry. Architectural materials—sawn timber, calibrated stone tiles, aluminum extrusions, and heavy power tools—feature chamfered, slightly softened edges rather than aggressive organic pills or fragile acute needles.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Chips):** `0.25rem` (4px). Provides a disciplined, precise industrial chamfer.
- **Medium Panels (Cards, Material Swatches, Data Modules):** `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers controlled, modern polish without breaking the geometric grid.
- **Large Overlays (Modals, Slide-over Specification Sheets):** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Prohibited:** Full pill shapes (`9999px`) are forbidden except for live status indicator dots. High-radius curvature contradicts the structural architectural identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial:** Background `#1E232A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#1E232A`, radius `0.25rem`. On hover: background shifts to `#0F1216`, border to `#D97706` (providing an amber architectural edge glow).
- **Accent Commercial:** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#B45309`. On hover: `#B45309`.
- **Secondary Technical:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E232A`, border 1px solid `#E5E7EB`. On hover: background `#F9FAFB`, border `#1E232A`.
- **Sizing:** Fixed vertical standards: Large (48px), Default (40px), Compact (32px). Label font `Space Grotesk` with uppercase styling on compact variants.

### Material & Finish Cards
- Surface background `#FFFFFF`, surrounded by a 1px outline in `#E5E7EB`.
- Image aspect ratio locked to architectural landscape `16:10` or structural square `1:1`.
- On hover: elevation transitions smoothly to Level 2 (`150ms ease-out`), accompanied by a subtle 1px border accent shift to `#D97706`.
- Internal structure separates visual finish previews from tabular technical specs (e.g., PEI rating, slip resistance, thickness in mm, and origin).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E232A`, default border `1px solid #D1D5DB`, radius `0.25rem`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#1E232A` with an ambient inner ring `0 0 0 1px #1E232A`. No fuzzy browser-native rings.
- Label: Monospace-inspired `label-md` in uppercase, floating strictly above input containers for absolute spatial clarity.

### Technical Chips & Badges
- **Specification Chips:** Background `#F3F4F6`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#1E232A`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Commercial Status Badges (In Stock / Heavy Machinery / Imported):**
  - High Excellence / Verified: Background `#FEF3C7`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
  - Stock / Metric Active: Background `#ECFDF5`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square footprint (`18px x 18px`) with `2px` roundedness for checkboxes; circular (`18px`) for radios.
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px solid #9CA3AF`.
- Checked: Fill `#1E232A` with a micro `#D97706` amber checkmark or central core dot, affirming mechanical precision.

### Specialized Component: Technical Specification Matrix
- Alternating zebra backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` to `#F9FAFB`).
- Column headers rendered in `#1E232A` with `label-md` tracking.
- Border dividing cells: 1px continuous solid `#E5E7EB`.
- Values displayed in tabular figures (`Inter`) with primary dimensions highlighted via bold weights.
